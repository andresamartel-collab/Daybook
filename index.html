ℹ️ What do these badges mean?

Badges are small rewards for looking after yourself. You earn them by reaching your daily goals – each badge shows what to do underneath its name.

  • Grey badge: not earned yet. Tap it to open the card where you can earn it.
  • Colored badge with ✓: earned today – well done! Tap it for a little cheer.
  • “3 of 10 earned” shows how many you’ve collected today. The line below the badges shows your last 7 days and which badge to try next.
  • All badges start fresh at midnight, so you can earn them again tomorrow.

You don’t need all 10 every day – even 🌱 First step is a good start.
