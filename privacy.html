Daybook privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Daybook is a personal daily health tracker. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

What Daybook stores

The entries you make (water, food servings, steps, workouts, sleep, weight, mood, habits, notes, goals and settings) are saved only in your browser's storage on your own device.

What Daybook does not do

  • It does not send your entries to any server.
  • It does not use accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • It does not sell or share your data with anyone.

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your device's IP address as part of a normal web request.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser settings, permanently removes everything Daybook has stored.

Health notice

Daybook is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Stop exercising if you feel pain, dizziness, chest pain or shortness of breath, and ask your doctor which exercises suit you if you have a health condition.
